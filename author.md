## Why REFLEX

This website is a collection of my thoughts and experiences on supply chain security, turned into dip-in education.

The goal is to help developers grok security and supply chain security in a way that's developer-focused, without the security or compliance buzzwords.

The framework has six stages: Reconnaissance, Evaluate, Fortify, Limit, Expose and Exercise. Together they take you from thinking like an attacker to practising your response, as the [REFLEX overview](#index-md) explains.

Much of what's here is just a different take on good software engineering practice.
